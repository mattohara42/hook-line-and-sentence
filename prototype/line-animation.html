R1 — cast, line and reel motion

The question this is here to answer: is a Bezier line with a tension-driven control point enough, or does the line want a real rope simulation? Cast it, then drag the tension slider while it is reeling — that slider is the same 0–100 the tension meter already tracks. Tension in the game rises on mistakes only, never on slow typing.

Keyboard: space casts, any letter is a keystroke tug, enter reels a word in. Numbers come from CONFIG.anim; the curve, the arc and the tug spring come from logic.js. Both are the real files.
